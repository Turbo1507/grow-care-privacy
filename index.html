Privacy Policy

Grow & Care is a children's club in Nuanu, Bali. This policy explains how our WhatsApp assistant handles your information.

What we collect

When you message us on WhatsApp we receive your phone number, your WhatsApp profile name and the messages you send, including answers to enrollment and club tour questions (for example your child's name and age, the program you are interested in and your preferred visit time).

How we use it

Only to answer your questions, process Early Bird enrollment and club tour requests, and contact you about them. We do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing.

Where it is stored

Messages are processed by Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform) and stored in our secure database hosted by Cloudflare. Our team may read your messages to reply to you.

How long we keep it

We keep your messages and requests for as long as needed to handle your enrollment, and no longer than 2 years.

Your rights

You can ask us to show or delete your data at any time by writing to us on WhatsApp or by email: growandcare.unit@gmail.com.
